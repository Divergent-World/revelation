Ninety compartments were woven at Angers between 1377 and 1382 — the whole of Revelation, rendered as a sequence you could walk alongside. Twenty of them no longer exist.

This book rebuilds all ninety, in the original order, against the original anchors, and marks on every plate whether what you are looking at survives, survives only in fragments, or has been reconstructed. The complete text of the Revelation to John is set alongside, with the words of Christ in red.
